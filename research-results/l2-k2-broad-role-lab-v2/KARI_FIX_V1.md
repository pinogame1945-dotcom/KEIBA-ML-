## Scope

This provisional fix applies only to L1.5 danger races where the consensus-world gate alerts and one or more K2 novel horses are added to the candidate pool.

It does NOT apply to normal PASS_SEVEN_ONLY races.

## Provisional rule

For every K2 novel horse H in the danger race:

- TRIFECTA: A1 -> A2 -> H
- TRIFECTA: A2 -> A1 -> H

Use every K2 novel horse. Do not apply an additional K2 selector in this provisional rule.

Therefore the ticket count is 2 x number_of_K2_novel_horses.

A1/A2 remain the Seven-King anchor horses. K2 novel is the horse added from outside the Seven-King Top6 union by the K2 outsider path.

Current evidence supports the structural relationship itself:
Seven-King anchors occupy the first two seats, while K2 novel is used as a third-seat outsider.

This matches the project preference to avoid shrinking the playable race set merely to decorate ROI.
