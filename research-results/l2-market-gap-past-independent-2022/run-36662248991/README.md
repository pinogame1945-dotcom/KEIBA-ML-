# L2 Market Gap — Past Independent 2022

Train the market-aware LambdaRank model using 2021 only and evaluate 2022 only. The V2 frozen policy (model Top15, market-rank upgrade >=5, max 1 ticket) is applied without tuning on 2022. The full 2022 policy grid is diagnostic only and must not be promoted based on 2022. This test was conceived after inspecting 2025, so it is a historical independent check of that hypothesis, not a pristine forward holdout. 2026 remains sealed.
