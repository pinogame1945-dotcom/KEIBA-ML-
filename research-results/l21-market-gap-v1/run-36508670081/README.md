# L2.1 Market Gap V1

Seven-King ranking is computed without odds/popularity/payout. It is compared only afterward with historical final WIN odds.

2022-2024 are the development diagnostic period. 2025 is holdout confirmation and is not used to tune bucket boundaries. 2026 remains sealed.

Primary outputs:
- overview.csv: agreement/correlation by period and Gate segment
- ai-top1-market-rank.csv: where Seven-King Top1 sits in the market and its flat WIN ROI
- market-favorite-ai-rank.csv: how Seven-King treats the market favorite
- rank-gap.csv: all Seven-union horses grouped by AI-vs-market rank gap
- signals.csv: preregistered treasure/danger signal summaries
- k2-novel-market-rank.csv: K2 novel horses versus market rank
- race-summary.csv: compact per-race audit ledger
- excluded-races.csv: races excluded only because usable final WIN market had fewer than 3 horses

No Market Gap feature is promoted to Bet Router by this run.
